The famous entrepreneur in the real estate sector approached the studio with a request for the renovation of a historic villa in Barcelona.

The main goal was to develop an exclusive and unique design that would increase the villa's value and ensure its profitable sale with a good profit.

We already had experience in similar projects and were interested in ensuring that investments in design and renovation were profitable.

  • Calculation and assessment of the project's investment appeal.
  • Development of necessary documentation for obtaining permits.
  • Budget planning for the project.
  • Creation of a unique interior and landscape design.
  • Selection of materials, furniture, and lighting for the interior.

We commissioned contemporary designs by the likes of Tailor Kibby, Sam Lock, inspiring chandelier of Frederick Molenschot, Lindsey Adelman, Baxter, The Officine Gullo kitchen as a foil to the homeowners' collection of antiques and Old Master paintings.

The villa is filled with design icons: Green marble table by SolidNature, Corner sofa by Baxter, Lighting by Paul Matter, brass sconce by Apparatus and monk chairs of Tobia Scarpa, armachairs by De Sede, wall lamps by Draga&Aurel and oil painting by Ben Crace, and a lot of others.

These furnishings and decor items inherently carry an investment and added value to the interior.

Simultaneously, we employed professional yet cost-effective methods that visually enhance the interior finish with charm and gloss.
