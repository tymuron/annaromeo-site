BORA HEADQUATERS

Site: BALEARIC ISLANDS, SPAIN
Year: 2017
Project Area: 1355 m2
This relaxed resort-style home and work environment blends into its surrounding bucolic landscape through its use of form and locally inspired materials. It reflects a contemporary take on Mallorca's architecture.
The building is layered in the use of materials including plaster, stone, wooden pergolas and shutters, aluminum and large expanses of glass.
